5 Voices

One FM voice is a sound; nine is an instrument. Each channel has its own instrument, volume and pitch — the register file just repeats the same layout nine times (10–18, 20–28, 30–38). Play a chord on the keyboard and a voice allocator hands each held note a free channel, stealing the oldest when all nine are busy — exactly what an MSX-MUSIC driver does.

The nine channels give each its own instrument, level and pitch

Nine, then six

Nine is the melodic maximum. The next stage spends three of them: switch on rhythm mode and channels 7-9 become a drum kit, leaving six melodic voices. That is why so much MSX-MUSIC you hear is six-note polyphony plus drums.
