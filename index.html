Yamaha YM2413 — the FM chip of MSX-MUSIC

Phase 3 · FM PSG ↗ SCC ↗

Phase 3 — FM: the second operator. Now the modulator is live: it phase-modulates the carrier, and that is FM synthesis. Press Play, hold a key, and sweep the modulator's Multiple, Total level and Feedback: hear the timbre change and watch the carrier's harmonic bars grow. A 2:1 Multiple ratio rings like a bell. Flip show internals to see the phase the modulator injects.
