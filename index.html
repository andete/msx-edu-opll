Phase 6 · Rhythm PSG ↗ SCC ↗

Phase 6 — rhythm mode. One bit — 0E bit 5 — turns the OPLL from nine melodic voices into six melodic + five drums: channels 7-9 become Bass, Snare, Tom, Cymbal and Hi-Hat, each keyed by its own bit of 0E, voiced by fixed rhythm patches and a shared noise generator, mixed a touch louder (+3 dB). Press Play, flip Rhythm mode in the panel below, then hit the pads or run the demo beat — channels 1-6 stay melodic on the keyboard. The detailed widgets up top still follow channel 1.
