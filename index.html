Phase 5 · Nine voices PSG ↗ SCC ↗

Phase 5 — nine voices. The OPLL is nine independent FM channels. Press Play and hold a chord on the keyboard — each note is handed a free voice (oldest stolen when all nine are busy), so up to nine sound at once. The Voices mixer below gives every channel its own instrument, pitch and level, and a Key button to sound it directly — watch 10–18/20–28/30–38 light up per channel in the register file. The detailed widgets up top still follow channel 1.
