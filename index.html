Yamaha YM2413 — the FM chip of MSX-MUSIC

Phase 2 · envelope PSG ↗ SCC ↗

Phase 2 — the ADSR envelope. Still one pure sine (the modulator is silenced — FM arrives next), but now shaped by a real Attack · Decay · Sustain · Release envelope. Press Play, hold a key and watch the playhead climb, decay and hold; let go to hear it release. Note the tiny damp dip at the very start of every Key-On — an OPLL quirk.
