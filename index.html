Yamaha YM2413 — the FM chip of MSX-MUSIC

Phase 4 · Patch + ROM PSG ↗ SCC ↗

Phase 4 — the whole patch + the ROM gallery. The OPLL mostly lets you pick a sound, not build one: 15 instruments live in ROM, and only the User slot is the 8 bytes you edit. Press Play, click any tile in the Instrument gallery below to hear it, and compare their harmonic fingerprints. Hit copy → user ✎ on a ROM sound to drop its bytes into the user slot and shape it with the operator controls — that is how you learn an FM patch.
